## Proposal
1. **Name match.** A similar name also means one cleaned name contains the other (whole words, generic words such as FOOD/MARKET ignored, at least 3 characters) or the token-set similarity is at least 0.90, in addition to the current similarity of at least 0.8. 'COMPARE FOODS' and 'COMPARE FOODS NORTH TRYON' then match.
2. **Co-tenant exclusion only when the names do not match.** Today nothing authorized more than 365 days before the end counts. Now a *strong* name match (containment, token-set >= 0.90 or similarity >= 0.90) within 0.05 mile, or at the identical address, is the same store (a duplicate or re-registration) however long the two records overlapped. The other record must still be authorized after the end; a record that had already ended is an earlier record, not a successor. Everything else keeps the current windows.
