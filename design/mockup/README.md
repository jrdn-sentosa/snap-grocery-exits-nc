# Grocery Watch: website mockup (reference only)

Exported from the team's design canvas "Charlotte Grocery Risk App" (civic atlas theme).
These `.dc.html` files are design references for the D5 frontend, not runnable pages
(they depend on the design tool's runtime). Read them for layout, copy, colors and fonts.

Screens (see `canvas.json` for sizes):
- `Main.dc.html`: home: hero, Mecklenburg store map, store list, receipt-style store detail
- `Highest.dc.html`: highest-risk outreach list (shelf tags / table), rank by risk or people affected
- `Check.dc.html`: "Check a store" form and result, with a developer view of the /predict response
- `Methods.dc.html`: how the scores work, exit definition, pie of how authorizations ended, limitations
- `Mobile.dc.html`: phone view of the store detail

Theme tokens (from the canvas notes):
- Paper #F4EFE3, paper light #FFFDF7, paper rule #D8CFBA, ink #1C2B22, ink secondary #4A5A50
- Forest (primary) #1F5A3D; high risk (tomato) #B23A12; medium risk (mustard) #D9A441, text #8A5A06; low risk = hollow ink ring
- Notice band #F3E3B8; map shading (fewer to more exits) #F3E0A8 #E7B672 #D98654 #BC4B24 #86280D; no SNAP grocery #E1DBCF
- Fonts: Bagel Fat One (wordmark only), Instrument Serif (headings, italic for emphasis), Inter Tight (body),
  IBM Plex Mono (labels); receipt and shelf tags: Plex Mono + Fraunces numbers. Radius 4px. Rules, not cards.

Everything shown as store names, scores and resident counts in the mockup is SAMPLE DATA.
Values in [brackets] are placeholders.
